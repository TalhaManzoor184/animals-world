# Character Reference Requirements

Every image call attaches the reference for each character in the shot. Clean full-body sheets (`*_sheet.png`) are used when present, otherwise the board crops.

## Milo (Main hero)
- Look (locked): orange tabby cat with subtle darker orange stripes, white muzzle and chin, large expressive amber eyes, wearing black rectangular sunglasses and a plain red hoodie with white drawstrings
- Personality: confident, clever, mischievous, dramatic
- Voice: confident, smooth, slightly smug young male comedy voice, American accent (TTS voice: Puck)
- Reference in use: `references/characters/milo_crop.png`

## Bruno (Milo's best friend)
- Look (locked): golden retriever with fluffy golden fur, big happy brown eyes, wearing a blue baseball cap turned backwards and a blue bandana with white paw prints tied around the neck
- Personality: loyal, innocent, goofy
- Voice: friendly, innocent, cheerful male voice, a bit slow (TTS voice: Achird)
- Reference in use: `references/characters/bruno_crop.png`

## Rocky (The troublemaker)
- Look (locked): white goat with curved grey-brown horns and a pink nose, wearing black sunglasses, a black t-shirt and a thick gold chain necklace
- Personality: attitude, dramatic, overconfident
- Voice: deep, dramatic, overconfident male voice (TTS voice: Charon)
- Reference in use: `references/characters/rocky_crop.png`

## Chiku (Overreacts to everything)
- Look (locked): orange-red rooster with bright glossy orange-red feathers, a tall red comb and red wattles, wearing black sunglasses and a gold chain necklace
- Personality: loud, dramatic, funny
- Voice: loud, panicky, dramatic voice that cracks when shouting (TTS voice: Sadachbia)
- Reference in use: `references/characters/chiku_crop.png`

## Ducky (Comedy specialist)
- Look (locked): white Pekin duck with clean white feathers and a bright orange beak, wearing black sunglasses and a gold chain necklace
- Personality: energetic, funny, unpredictable
- Voice: high-energy, quick, squeaky funny voice (TTS voice: Fenrir)
- Reference in use: `references/characters/ducky_crop.png`
