# Concept Batch 01: 10 Episode Ideas

Scoring: Hook (first 3s), Entertainment, Replay, Shareability, Simplicity. Each is out of 5, for a total out of 25.
Every idea fits 3–4 Veo clips of 8s (24–32s) and needs at most 3 characters per shot, which keeps character consistency manageable.

### 1. Operation Watermelon ⭐ SELECTED (≈32s)
- **Concept:** Milo plans an elaborate spy-style heist to grab a giant watermelon from the picnic table. Every teammate ruins the plan, but it still works out for someone.
- **Hook:** Extreme close-up of Milo peeking over a hay bale in sunglasses, whispering *"Operation Watermelon… starts now."*
- **Comedy moment:** Rocky struts in to "show the professionals," his sunglasses slip, he bumps the table, and the watermelon rolls away. Chiku screams *"IT'S ESCAPING!"*
- **Punchline:** The watermelon rolls into a rock, cracks open right in front of Ducky, who is lounging in the grass. *"Thanks for the delivery!"* Milo slowly lowers his sunglasses.
- **Viral potential:** High. The heist parody is instantly readable, the twist is visual, the ending rewards a rewatch, and it shows four characters, which works as a squad intro for a new channel.

### 2. The Selfie Disaster (≈24s)
- **Concept:** Rocky tries to take the perfect "cool" selfie with a phone propped on a fence. Ducky photobombs every shot with a more ridiculous pose.
- **Hook:** Rocky: *"Okay. Serious face. Model mode."* Ducky's head pops into the frame.
- **Comedy:** Three quick shots, each photobomb bigger than the last.
- **Punchline:** The final photo goes "viral" on the farm because only Ducky is in frame. Rocky just has a horn tip in the corner.
- **Viral:** High relatability, but it is a 2-character piece, so it is better as episode 2 or 3.

### 3. Milo Ordered a Box (≈24s)
- **Concept:** A big delivery box arrives. Milo hypes it as "the best purchase of my life." Bruno is dying to know what's inside.
- **Hook:** Milo dramatically pats a giant cardboard box: *"Bruno… this changes everything."*
- **Punchline:** The box is empty. Milo climbs inside, perfectly content: *"Best purchase ever."*
- **Viral:** "Cats love boxes" is a universal meme, so it should share well with cat owners.

### 4. Chiku's Alarm Clock Strike (≈24s)
- **Concept:** Chiku announces he is done waking everyone up. The squad celebrates their first sleep-in.
- **Hook:** Chiku in sunglasses at dawn: *"I quit. No more cock-a-doodle."*
- **Punchline:** Everyone is asleep at noon, and Chiku panics and screams the loudest crow ever: *"I CAN'T DO THIS!"*
- **Viral:** Strong sound gag, and Chiku's overreaction makes good loop material.

### 5. Bruno Learns "Play Dead" (≈24s)
- **Concept:** Milo trains Bruno for a talent show. Bruno takes every command way too literally.
- **Punchline:** On "play dead," Bruno flops dramatically and then starts snoring for real. Coco gives him a 10/10 scorecard.
- **Viral:** Dog-trick comedy is evergreen.

### 6. Coco's Magic Trick (≈24s)
- **Concept:** Coco makes Rocky's sunglasses "disappear" with a cloth.
- **Punchline:** Rocky is begging for them back, and the camera tilts up to show Coco is wearing them.
- **Viral:** A clean visual gag with no dialogue needed, so it works internationally.

### 7. The Talent Show Judge (≈28s)
- **Concept:** Milo is a stern judge with a clipboard. Ducky and Chiku perform terrible acts.
- **Punchline:** Milo gives Chiku a perfect score only because the screaming finally stopped.
- **Viral:** Parody of TV talent shows, and the format can recur.

### 8. Rocky Opens a Gym (≈24s)
- **Concept:** Rocky sells Bruno "premium membership" to a gym made of hay bales.
- **Punchline:** Bruno lifts the "weights" with one paw. They're hay bales. Rocky: *"…That's the advanced plan."*
- **Viral:** Medium; the gym-bro parody lands with a US audience.

### 9. Who Ate the Carrot Cake? (≈32s)
- **Concept:** An interrogation-room parody with Milo as detective, questioning each animal under a lamp.
- **Punchline:** Coco swears innocence with frosting on her whiskers.
- **Viral:** Medium-high, but five characters in one episode is harder to keep consistent, so save it for when the reference sheets are proven.

### 10. Ducky's Swimming Lessons (≈24s)
- **Concept:** Ducky "teaches" Bruno to swim in a kiddie pool.
- **Punchline:** Bruno is a natural. Ducky sinks dramatically (in shallow water, fully safe) and pops up: *"I'm a land duck now."*
- **Viral:** Medium; the water physics may be harder for Veo to render.

---
**Why #1 first:** It introduces four of six characters and their personalities in one short (the leader, the goofball, the show-off, the screamer and the lucky one), which makes it the best pilot for a new channel. The twist is purely visual, so it works without sound too.
