# Operation Watermelon: Full Script

## Scene 1: HOOK: the plan  [00.00s – 07.00s]
*Milo peeks over the top of a golden hay bale in the foreground, only his head, paws and the top of his red hoodie visible, leaning toward the camera conspiratorially. Bruno sits just behind Milo's right shoulder, head poking up beside him, ears perked, tongue slightly out.*

**Action:** Milo slowly turns his head toward camera and leans in to whisper; Bruno bounces up excitedly and shouts with his tail wagging; Milo snaps his head toward Bruno and raises one paw to his mouth to shush him.

**MILO** *(whispered, dramatic, secret-agent)*: "Team. Operation Watermelon... starts now."  `@00.40s`
**BRUNO** *(way too loud, happy)*: "YES, BOSS!"  `@04.20s`
**MILO** *(sharp, annoyed whisper)*: "Shhh!"  `@05.90s`

**SFX:** soft rustling hay, a distant rooster cluck, a quiet breeze  
**Camera:** eye-level with the animals, 50mm lens, extreme close-up on Milo with Bruno slightly behind; very slow push-in on Milo

## Scene 2: SETUP: the professional  [07.00s – 14.00s]
*Rocky stands confidently in the open grass in the foreground, chest out, chin up, mid-stride as if he just stepped into frame. Milo peeks from behind the hay bale on the left edge in the background.*

**Action:** Rocky turns his head toward Milo and speaks, then struts in slow motion toward the picnic table with exaggerated swagger, his gold chain swinging; in the background Milo slowly turns his face to the camera with a deadpan look.

**ROCKY** *(deep, smug, dramatic)*: "Step aside, amateurs. Watch a professional."  `@07.30s`

**SFX:** confident hoof steps on grass, gold chain clinking  
**Camera:** low angle from grass level, 35mm lens, medium-full shot making Rocky look heroic; slow tracking shot following Rocky from behind as he walks toward the table

## Scene 3: CONFLICT: the watermelon escapes  [14.00s – 22.00s]
*Rocky stands right beside the wooden picnic table, head tilted in a 'cool' pose, his sunglasses sliding down his nose. The giant green striped watermelon sits on the edge of the table next to him. Chiku stands on top of the wooden fence in the background.*

**Action:** Rocky flicks his head in a cool pose, his sunglasses slip down his nose and he leans to catch them, gently nudging the table with his shoulder; the watermelon wobbles, drops softly onto the grass and starts rolling down the slope; Rocky freezes with an open-mouthed stare; on the fence Chiku flaps his wings wildly and screams.

**CHIKU** *(loud, panicked, dramatic)*: "THE WATERMELON IS ESCAPING!"  `@18.00s`

**SFX:** wooden table bump, soft thud, rolling rumble on grass, frantic wing flaps  
**Camera:** eye-level, 35mm lens, medium-wide shot; quick pan right following the rolling watermelon

## Scene 4: PUNCHLINE: the delivery  [22.00s – 29.00s]
*Ducky lounges relaxed in the soft grass at the bottom of the slope next to a small smooth rock, wings resting behind his head like a sunbather. The giant watermelon is rolling toward him from the upper left.*

**Action:** the watermelon rolls into the small rock and cracks open neatly into two halves showing bright red flesh, right in front of Ducky; Ducky slowly lowers his sunglasses, looks at the watermelon, grins widely and speaks happily, then takes a little peck of the red fruit.

**DUCKY** *(delighted, high energy)*: "Ooh! Thanks for the delivery!"  `@25.00s`

**SFX:** satisfying crack of watermelon, juicy crunch, happy quack  
**Camera:** low angle at grass level, 35mm lens, medium shot; slow push-in on Ducky

## Scene 5: TAG: Milo's stare (loop point)  [29.00s – 33.00s]
*Milo and Bruno stand side by side at the top of the slope looking down toward the camera, Milo in front.*

**Action:** Milo very slowly lowers his sunglasses with one paw and stares straight into the camera without blinking; Bruno tilts his head and asks his question.

**BRUNO** *(innocent, unsure)*: "...Mission success?"  `@30.80s`

**SFX:** a single cricket chirp in the silence  
**Camera:** eye-level, 85mm lens, medium close-up, symmetrical; fast dramatic zoom-in on Milo's face

**Music:** Light comedic 'sneaky spy' pizzicato loop for clips 1-2, a quick rise for clip 3, a stop-down beat on the punchline, then a tiny comedic sting under the Milo stare.
