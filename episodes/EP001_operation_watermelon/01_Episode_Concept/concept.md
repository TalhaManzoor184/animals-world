# EP001: Operation Watermelon
**Category:** Food stealing + unexpected plot twist
**Main:** Milo | **Supporting:** Bruno, Rocky, Chiku, Ducky
**Target duration:** 33.0s (9:16, 1080x1920)

**Story:** Milo leads a spy-style heist to grab a giant watermelon from the farm picnic table. Bruno blows their cover, Rocky takes over to 'show the professionals' and knocks the watermelon off the table, and Chiku panics as it rolls away. It cracks open right in front of Ducky, who thanks everyone for the delivery.

**Hook (0-3s):** Extreme close-up of Milo in sunglasses peeking over a hay bale, whispering the mission name.

**Main comedy moment:** Rocky's cool strut ends with his sunglasses slipping and a gentle bump that sends the watermelon rolling across the yard.

**Punchline:** The watermelon cracks open at Ducky's feet: 'Thanks for the delivery!' Milo slowly lowers his sunglasses in disbelief.

**Viral potential:** Heist parody that reads instantly, visual twist that works on mute, and a loopable ending (Milo's stare leads back into his opening whisper).
